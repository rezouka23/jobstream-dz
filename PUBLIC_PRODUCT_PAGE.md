Fresh Algeria job opportunities, delivered to Telegram in clean, fast digests.

## Product Overview

JobStream DZ was built to solve one simple problem: finding fresh jobs in Algeria is scattered and time-consuming. Instead of checking many websites manually, users get curated updates directly in one Telegram channel.

- Channel: `@jobstream_dz`
- Format: digest post with 5 jobs per batch
- Freshness-first flow with duplicate protection
- Multi-source aggregation through one unified publishing stream

---

## Key Features

- Fresh job monitoring from a centralized Google Sheets pipeline
- Clean Telegram digests with role, company, location, time, and apply link
- Duplicate prevention using persistent job fingerprinting
- Scheduled polling every 3 minutes
- Reliable runtime with PM2 process supervision and restart support
- Human-friendly, mobile-native delivery where users already spend time

---

## Why Users Follow JobStream DZ

- Save time by avoiding manual multi-site searches
- Catch new jobs early and apply faster
- Reduce noise from repeated listings
- Consume updates quickly from phone-friendly Telegram messages

---

## How It Works

1. New rows are read from the jobs sheet.
2. Unseen jobs are detected using unique fingerprints.
3. The bot waits until at least 5 unseen jobs are available.
4. One digest message is published to Telegram with exactly 5 jobs.
5. Posted jobs are recorded to prevent reposting.

## Promotion Map

## Phase 1: Positioning and Launch Assets (Week 1)

- Define clear value proposition: "Fresh Algeria jobs, no spam, no duplicates."
- Prepare visual kit: logo, post templates, cover image, and short intro video.
- Create pinned channel post with what users get and how often.
- Publish a simple landing page with channel link and social proof section.

## Phase 2: Launch Campaign (Weeks 2-3)

- Launch announcement on LinkedIn, Facebook, and X with channel invite link.
- Share in relevant Algeria job groups and university communities.
- Ask 10-20 trusted early users to join and share feedback/testimonials.
- Run a 7-day content streak: one daily post explaining a feature or benefit.

## Phase 3: Growth Engine (Month 2)

- Weekly recap posts: top sectors hiring, top cities, top roles.
- Partner with career pages, student clubs, and training centers for cross-promo.
- Add referral CTA in digest footer: "Invite a friend searching for work."
- Publish success stories: people who got interviews through the channel.

## Phase 4: Scale and Retention (Month 3+)

- Segment content by interests: remote roles, internships, tech, support, bilingual.
- Launch regional micro-campaigns for major cities.
- Test paid boosts for best-performing social posts.
- Add monthly analytics post to build trust and transparency.
